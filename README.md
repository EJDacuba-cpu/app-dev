# My Favorite Series: Sweet Home

## Overview
**Sweet Home** is a South Korean horror-thriller series based on the popular webtoon of the same name.  
It tells the story of residents trapped inside an apartment building during a mysterious monster apocalypse.

---

##  Why I Love This Series
1. The intense **emotions** and deep **character development**.
2. The unique mix of **horror**, **drama**, and **survival themes**.
3. The visual effects and **monster designs** are top-notch!
